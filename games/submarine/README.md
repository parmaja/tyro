# Submarine

A retro deep-sea rescue arcade for Tyro, in the spirit of the eighties
cabinets. You captain a small yellow research submarine and six divers are
lost somewhere in the dark below. Swim down, pick them up and carry every
single one of them back to the surface alive.

The catch: your oxygen tank burns all the time, and only the top of the
sea refills it. Sharks patrol the mid water, jellyfish drift up from the
floor, floating mines pop out of the depths and, from level 3, a dark red
patrol sub hunts you and drops mines right at your depth. Your torpedoes
clear the way — a shark is one hit, a mine is worth more, and the patrol
sub is armored.

Rescue all six divers to clear a level. Every level is deeper, darker and
busier than the last, cycling through four water palettes (tropical
shallows, sunset shelf, cold depths, midnight trench).

## Run

```
tyro games/submarine/submarine.tyro
```

All assets load from the script folder: `img/` (sprites + `.sprite`
atlases) and `sfx/` (WAV effects). The water shader `underwater.frag`
must stay next to the script as well.

## Controls

| Input | Action |
|-------|--------|
| `LEFT` / `RIGHT` or `A` / `D` | cruise |
| `UP` / `DOWN` or `W` / `S` | depth |
| `SPACE` or `ENTER` | fire torpedo |
| `P` | pause |
| `M` | mute / unmute |
| `Q` / `E` | water shader strength |
| `ESC` | quit |

A gamepad works too: stick or d-pad moves, `A` fires.

## How it plays

- **Oxygen**: the O2 bar drains anywhere below the waterline and only
  refills while you surface. Dip too deep for too long and the tank runs
  dry — that counts as a hit.
- **Rescue**: divers swim around at random depths. Touch one to collect
  it (+50), then surface: each diver is deposited at the waterline one by
  one (+100 each). The HUD counters show banked/needed divers.
- **Threats**: sharks (60 pts), jellyfish (40), mines (90) and the
  armored patrol sub (invulnerable — ram it at your peril). Touching any
  of them costs a life and scatters your carried divers.
- **Treasure**: a chest floats up from the sea floor now and then
  (+250 and a small O2 sip).
- **Torpedoes**: one in the tube at a time, fired straight ahead.
- **Levels**: each level deepens the palette, rising shader strength
  feel included, more enemies, faster sharks, mines and jellyfish from
  level 2, the patrol sub from level 3, sniffing deep-sharks from
  level 4. Clear bonus: 400 + 100 per level.

## Attract mode

Create an empty `attract.txt` file next to the script (any content) and
the title screen flips into a classic arcade attract demo: an autopilot
"demo captain" hunts divers, dodges sharks and plays by itself. Delete
the file to play normally. This is also how automated tests play the
game.

## Art & sound

Everything under `img/` and `sfx/` is **original, generated art**: run

```
python games/submarine/make_assets.py
```

to regenerate all sprite sheets, `.sprite` atlases (ZIP + PNG + JSON
frames) and the ten WAV sound effects with Pillow + the standard-library
`wave` module. No external assets are used.

The underwater look comes from `underwater.frag`, a custom GLSL shader
applied to the whole canvas that adds wobble (growing with depth),
moving caustic light bands, a depth tint, scanlines and a vignette.
